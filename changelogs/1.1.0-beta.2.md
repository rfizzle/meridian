This beta brings a massive wave of new enchantments across nearly every item type, rounding out the enchantment pool with dozens of additions — weapons, tools, armor, shields, elytras, mounts, tridents, ranged weapons, utility slots, and economy curses all got new options. A spyglass enchanting surface also makes its debut.

## Added

- **Weapon enchantments:** Reap, Mark, and Volley give melee and ranged weapons new tactical options.
- **Tool enchantments:** Kiln, Meticulous, Trailblaze, Dowse, and Twin Hook expand what your tools can do in the world.
- **Armor enchantments:** Winterward, Umbral, and Stormward add new defensive and elemental protections.
- **Shield enchantments:** Stagger and Bullrush turn your shield into an active threat.
- **Elytra enchantments:** Tailwind and Falconstrike make aerial traversal and diving attacks more powerful.
- **Mount enchantments:** Wavestride and Endurance improve riding in water and over long distances.
- **Trident enchantments:** Undertow and Torrent bring new aquatic combat and movement options to tridents.
- **Ranged enchantments:** Pin and Skyfall add crowd-control and aerial sniper play.
- **Utility enchantments:** Groom, Verdure, and Clearsight cover quality-of-life niches that were previously empty.
- **Economy enchantments:** Thrift and Alchemist's Draw reduce costs; Curse of Toll and Curse of Dissonance add new economic downsides to the curse pool.
- **Aquatic enchantments:** Ballast and Abyssal, plus the new Curse of Waterlogging, fill out the underwater kit.
- **Attunement** — a shelf-bound repair enchantment that links an item to a nearby shelf for passive mending.
- **Tracker's Lens** — the spyglass is now an enchantable item with its own enchantment surface.
- **Eleven new curses** fill gaps across item types that previously had no curse coverage.
- A `/meridian audit` command lets you inspect how third-party enchantments are registered alongside Meridian's own.

## Fixed

- Curse of Molting no longer causes a visible flicker when the fizzle effect is predicted client-side.
