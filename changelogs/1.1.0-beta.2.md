This beta is all about enchantments — a major expansion of what you can find on the enchanting table, covering every item type from weapons and tools to shields, tridents, mounts, and even spyglasses. A handful of economy and curse enchantments round out the pool.

## Added

- **Weapons:** Reap, Mark, and Volley offer new combat options for melee and ranged fighters.
- **Tools:** Kiln, Meticulous, Trailblaze, Dowse, and Twin Hook expand the crafting and exploration toolkit.
- **Armor:** Winterward, Umbral, and Stormward add protective options for different environments and situations.
- **Shields & Elytra:** Stagger and Bullrush for shields; Tailwind and Falconstrike for elytra.
- **Tridents & Ranged:** Undertow and Torrent for tridents; Pin and Skyfall for bows and crossbows.
- **Mounts & Utility:** Wavestride and Endurance for mounts; Groom, Verdure, and Clearsight as general utility picks.
- **Economy:** Thrift and Alchemist's Draw reduce costs in different ways; Curse of Toll and Curse of Dissonance add new economy-targeting curses.
- **Aquatic:** Ballast and Abyssal for underwater play, alongside the new Curse of Waterlogging.
- **Spyglass:** Spyglasses are now enchantable — Tracker's Lens is the first enchantment for them.
- **Attunement:** A new shelf-bound repair enchantment that mends items using the shelf network.
- **Curses:** Eleven new curses spread across item types that previously had no curse coverage.
- **Audit command:** A new client-side command lets you inspect third-party enchantments registered by other mods.

## Fixed

- Curse of Molting no longer shows a brief visual glitch (fizzle flicker) on the client when the effect is cancelled server-side.
