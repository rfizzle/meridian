Shelves & Setups

All shelf blocks, their stat contributions, and recommended combinations for reaching key enchanting thresholds.

Stat Primer

Shelf blocks contribute five independent stats to the enchanting table. Here is a quick recap of what each one does.

Eterna

Maximum enchanting level (0–50). Higher Eterna unlocks stronger enchantments and table crafting recipes.

Quanta

Power variance (0–100). High Quanta means bigger swings — potentially amazing or terrible results.

Arcana

Rarity bias (0–100). Higher Arcana shifts the pool toward rarer enchantments. Required for many recipes.

Rectification

Counters Quanta's negative variance. High Rectification + high Quanta = consistently strong enchantments.

Clues

Reveals extra enchantments in the tooltip preview before you commit. Each point shows one more result.

Utility Shelves

These shelves have special behaviors beyond raw stat contributions. They occupy a shelf slot but serve a unique purpose.

Filtering Shelf

A block entity that lets players blacklist specific enchantments from appearing at the enchanting table. Right-click to open the filtering UI and select which enchantments to exclude.

Stats identical to a vanilla bookshelf (Eterna +1, maxEterna 15). Uses a shelf slot but adds filtering control.

Treasure Shelf

A block entity that unlocks treasure-tier enchantments (e.g., Mending) at the enchanting table. Without a Treasure Shelf placed nearby (or a config override), treasure enchantments will not appear in the enchanting pool.

Essential for accessing the most valuable vanilla and Meridian enchantments through the table.

Sightshelf / Sightshelf T2

Pure Clues providers. They contribute no other stats — their only purpose is to reveal additional enchantments in the tooltip preview before you spend levels. Sightshelf gives +1 Clue, Sightshelf T2 gives +2 Clues.

Reduces blind gambling. Pair with other shelves that provide the stats you need.

Rectifier / T2 / T3

Pure Rectification providers. They contribute no Eterna, Quanta, or Arcana — they only counter the negative variance introduced by Quanta. Higher tiers give more Rectification per slot: +10, +15, or +25.

Essential when using high-Quanta shelves like Blazing Hellshelves or Soul-Touched variants.

Stoneshelf

Reduces Arcana and Eterna. Useful when you want to lower Arcana to avoid rare enchantments and target common ones instead. Gives -1.5 Eterna and -7.5 Arcana per shelf.

Niche but valuable for targeted enchanting setups.

Beeshelf & Melonshelf

Stat manipulators. Beeshelf gives a massive +100 Quanta but -15 Eterna — extreme variance at the cost of level cap. Melonshelf gives -10 Quanta and -1 Eterna — use it to reduce Quanta when your setup has too much.

Beeshelf is for players who want to gamble. Melonshelf is the antidote.

Recommended Setups

The enchanting table supports up to 15 shelf blocks in the standard 5x5 ring (same positions as vanilla bookshelves). Below are practical shelf combinations for reaching key stat thresholds at each stage of progression. Check in-game tooltips for exact stat totals — these are guidelines, not exact math.

Vanilla Start

Eterna 15

Shelves

15x Vanilla Bookshelf

Approximate Stats

Eterna 15 Quanta 0 Arcana 0

Unlocks

Basic enchanting, same as vanilla Minecraft. Golden Carrot crafting. Tier-1 XP Bottle crafting (with enough Quanta and Arcana from non-shelf sources).

Tip: This is your starting point. No Quanta means no variance — results are predictable but capped at vanilla power levels. Swap shelves out one at a time as you acquire Nether and Ocean materials.

Early Nether / Ocean

Eterna ~22

Shelves (pick one path)

Nether Path (Quanta)

15x Hellshelf

Eterna ~22.5, Quanta ~45, Arcana 0

Ocean Path (Arcana)

15x Seashelf

Eterna ~22.5, Quanta 0, Arcana ~30

Unlocks

Infused Hellshelf and Infused Seashelf upgrades. Improved Scrap Tome crafting (with sufficient Arcana). Stronger enchantments beyond vanilla level 30.

Tip: Hellshelves add high Quanta — results become volatile. Consider mixing in 1–2 Rectifiers early to tame the variance. Seashelves are more stable but give Arcana instead of Quanta, which shifts the enchantment pool toward rarer results.

Mid-Game Power

Eterna ~30

Shelves

Mix of Blazing Hellshelves (high Eterna + Quanta) and Crystal Seashelves (balanced Quanta + Arcana). Add Rectifiers to counter the Quanta variance and Sightshelves for preview visibility.

Example: 8x Blazing Hellshelf + 3x Crystal Seashelf + 2x Rectifier + 1x Sightshelf T2 + 1x Treasure Shelf

Approximate Stats

Eterna ~30 Quanta high Arcana moderate Rectification +20 Clues +2

Unlocks

Deepshelf awakening (Dormant Deepshelf to Deepshelf). Extraction Tome crafting. Budding Amethyst transmutation. 8x XP Bottle crafting. Most mid-tier table recipes.

Tip: This is where Rectifiers become critical. Blazing Hellshelves add +5 Quanta each, and without Rectification your results will swing wildly. Note that Blazing Hellshelves give -1 Clue each, so Sightshelves help compensate. Add a Treasure Shelf if you want Mending and other treasure enchantments to appear.

Deep Dark

Eterna ~37

Shelves

Core of Deepshelves, Echoing Deepshelves (Arcana focus), and Soul-Touched Deepshelves (Quanta focus). Add Echoing Sculkshelves or Soul-Touched Sculkshelves to push higher.

Example: 5x Echoing Sculkshelf + 4x Echoing Deepshelf + 3x Soul-Touched Sculkshelf + 2x Rectifier T2 + 1x Sightshelf T2

Approximate Stats

Eterna ~37 Quanta high Arcana very high Rectification +45 Clues +7

Unlocks

Infused Breath crafting (Dragon's Breath → 3x Infused Breath at E≥40). Echo Shard duplication. Access to the highest non-End enchantment tiers.

Tip: Sculkshelves are the powerhouses of this tier — 5 Eterna each with maxEterna 40. Echoing Sculkshelves provide +1 Clue to help compensate slot usage on Sightshelves. Soul-Touched Sculkshelves include built-in +5 Rectification, reducing the need for dedicated Rectifier slots.

End-Game Maximum

Eterna 50

Shelves

Draconic Endshelves are required — they are the only shelf with maxEterna 50, making them the only way to reach the Eterna cap. Fill remaining slots with Pearl Endshelves for balanced Quanta/Arcana, and utility shelves as needed.

Example: 5x Draconic Endshelf + 6x Pearl Endshelf + 2x Rectifier T2 + 1x Sightshelf T2 + 1x Treasure Shelf

Approximate Stats

Eterna 50 Quanta ~45 Arcana ~45 Rectification +30 Clues +2

Unlocks

32x XP Bottle crafting. Maximum enchantment power for all enchantments. Required Eterna level for the Ender Library upgrade (alongside extreme Arcana).

Tip: Draconic Endshelves give +10 Eterna each but zero Quanta/Arcana. They are pure power. Pearl Endshelves give +5 Eterna with +7.5 Quanta and +7.5 Arcana each — use them to build up the other stats. You need Draconic Endshelves to push beyond Eterna 45.

Ender Library Upgrade

E=50 / Q=45–50 / A=100

Shelves

This is the hardest recipe in the mod. You need Eterna exactly 50, Quanta between 45 and 50, and Arcana exactly 100. Reaching Arcana 100 requires massive Arcana contributors: Echoing Deepshelves (+15 Arcana each), Echoing Sculkshelves (+15 Arcana each), and Heart Seashelves (+10 Arcana each).

You must carefully balance the 15 slots to hit all three thresholds simultaneously. Draconic Endshelves are needed for Eterna 50, but they give zero Arcana. Every non-Draconic slot must maximize Arcana contribution.

Strategy

  • Use the minimum number of Draconic Endshelves needed to reach Eterna 50
  • Fill remaining slots with Echoing Deepshelves and Echoing Sculkshelves for maximum Arcana
  • Heart Seashelves provide +10 Arcana but come with -5 Rectification — factor that in
  • Use Stoneshelves to reduce Arcana if you overshoot (need exactly 100)
  • Quanta must land in the 45–50 window — Skulls and Soul-Touched shelves help here
  • Experiment in-game with the tooltip to fine-tune your exact layout

Tip: This is an aspirational end-game goal. The Ender Library doubles the point cap of the Basic Library (level 31 vs. level 16) and preserves all stored enchantment data during the upgrade. Plan your shelf layout carefully — trial and error at the table is expected.

Data-Driven Customization

All shelf stats are loaded from datapacks, not hardcoded. You can override any shelf's stat contribution without touching the mod jar.

Overriding Shelf Stats

Shelf stat contributions are defined as JSON files in data/meridian/enchanting_stats/ within any datapack. To change what stats a shelf block provides, create a datapack that overrides the relevant JSON file.

For example, to make Hellshelves provide more Eterna, create a file at data/meridian/enchanting_stats/hellshelf.json in your datapack and adjust the values.

After making changes, run /reload (the vanilla datapack reload command) to apply them without restarting the server. Changes take effect immediately for all players.
